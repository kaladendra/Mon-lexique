# Mon lexique

A spaced-repetition vocabulary drill for the Government of Canada **Second
Language Evaluation** (SLE / ÉLS) in French — built around the Canada School of
Public Service's own A/B lexicon.

**[Open the app →](https://kaladendra.github.io/Mon-lexique/)**

One self-contained HTML file. No account, no install, no tracking. Works
offline; your progress stays in your browser.

**Want it offline?** Download **`index.html`** — that one file is the whole app.
Rename it to anything you like and double-click it. Everything else in this
repository is either supporting material or icons the browser fetches on its
own; you don't need any of it.

## Keep it on your phone

It works as a home-screen app — full screen, no browser bars, and **offline once you've
opened it once** (it caches itself on first visit).

**iPhone / iPad** (Safari): open the link → **Share** button → scroll down →
**Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

**Android** (Chrome): ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

Do this *before* you start drilling. Your progress is stored by whichever
browser you used, so a home-screen copy added later may start from scratch —
and each device keeps its own progress regardless.

## What's in it

**2,861 cards**, organised the way the CSPS program is:

| List | Cards | |
|---|---|---|
| **Priorité** | yours | Starts empty. Press ★ on any card to add it. |
| **Boîte à outils** | 292 | The frames, connectors and repair phrases that carry an oral interview. |
| **Mes erreurs** | auto | Fills itself once you've missed a card three times, and empties as you improve. |
| **Faux amis** | 70 | The words that look English and mean something else. |
| **Niveau A** — objectives 1–32 | ~2,000 | The 40 CSPS training objectives, each selectable on its own. |
| **Niveau B** — objectives 33–40 | ~300 | Officially the Level B content: expressing choice, proposing solutions, forecasting, explaining decisions, persuading. |

## How it drills

- **English → French by default.** The oral test scores what you can *produce*, not what you recognise — and recognition is the easier direction, so it flatters you.
- **Three verdicts, not two.** A missing accent or a typo is *Presque* — it counts as a pass but the card returns sooner. Only a real miss is a miss.
- **Spaced repetition** (SM-2): 1 day, 3 days, then widening intervals. Cards you know drop out of rotation by themselves, which is why 2,861 cards stays manageable without triaging anything by hand.
- **Interface in French or English**, toggled in the corner.

## Sources

Vocabulary from the Canada School of Public Service: the *Lexique — Programme
de français langue seconde, Niveaux A et B* (2005) and the *aide-mémoire
linguistiques* of course **C285** (2011). Both are public, archived Government
of Canada publications.

Not affiliated with, nor endorsed by, the School or the Public Service
Commission. **Contains no protected test content** — actual SLE test material is
confidential, and none of it is here.
