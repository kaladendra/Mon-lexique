==============================================================================
  MON LEXIQUE
  A vocabulary drill for the Government of Canada Second Language Evaluation
  (SLE / ELS).
==============================================================================

  Open the app:  https://kaladendra.github.io/Mon-lexique/

  One self-contained HTML file. No account, no install, no tracking.
  Works offline; your progress stays in your browser.

  WANT IT OFFLINE?  Download index.html - that one file is the whole app.
  Rename it to anything you like and double-click it. Everything else here
  is supporting material or icons the browser fetches by itself; you do not
  need any of it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S IN IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  2,861 cards, organised the way the CSPS program is:

  Priorite ................ yours to build
      Starts empty. Press the star on any card (or the S key) to add it.

  Boite a outils .......... 292 cards
      The frames, connectors and repair phrases that carry an oral
      interview - sequencing, comparing, conceding, repairing.

  Mes erreurs ............. fills automatically
      Appears once you have missed a card three times, and empties again
      as you improve.

  Faux amis ............... 70 cards
      The words that look English and mean something else.

  Niveau A ................ objectives 1-32, about 2,000 cards
      The CSPS training objectives, each selectable on its own.

  Niveau B ................ objectives 33-40, about 300 cards
      Officially the Level B content: expressing choice, proposing
      solutions, forecasting, explaining decisions, persuading.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT DRILLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  English to French by default.
      The oral test scores what you can PRODUCE, not what you recognise -
      and recognition is the easier direction, so it flatters you.

  Three verdicts, not two.
      A missing accent or a typo is "Presque": it counts as a pass, but the
      card returns sooner. Only a real miss is a miss.

  Spaced repetition (SM-2).
      1 day, 3 days, then widening intervals. Cards you know drop out of
      rotation by themselves - which is why 2,861 cards stays manageable
      without triaging anything by hand.

  Interface in French or English, toggled in the corner.
